KA

Data Analytics · SQL, Excel & Power BI

Hi, I’m Kelys.

I started my career in law and public administration in Cuba. I reviewed official records for accuracy and compliance in civil registration, then worked as an attorney, where I reviewed case files, weighed evidence, and explained what it meant for the people involved. Both roles taught me how much a decision depends on the quality of the information behind it.

That experience shapes the way I approach data analytics today. I’m pursuing a B.S. in Data Analytics at Western Governors University and use SQL, Excel, and Power BI to investigate practical questions. In my projects, I’ve examined how often flights leave DFW late, which support tickets take the longest to resolve, and why product categories with similar sales can deliver very different profits.

What I enjoy most is turning those findings into something people can use. I start with a question, check how the data was collected and what it can support, and explain the result in plain language.

Projects

DFW Flight Delays 2025

Using SQL and Power BI, I analyzed 315,854 flights scheduled to depart from DFW in 2025.

28.58% of non-canceled departures were delayed by at least 15 minutes.

IT Service Desk Dashboard

I built a Power BI dashboard to track resolution times, ticket status, and user satisfaction across 100 IT support tickets.

Access and login tickets took the longest to resolve, averaging 12 hours compared with 6.5 hours overall.

View project on GitHub

Superstore Sales Dashboard

I built a Power BI dashboard to analyze $2.30 million in sales across product categories, regions, and customer segments.

Technology and Furniture had similar sales, but Technology generated about $145,000 in profit compared with about $18,000 for Furniture.
